About

I'm a first year Master's student studying Computer Science at UCLA. I completed my undergrad at UCLA as well, with a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science with a minor in Data Science. I'm primarily interested in computer microarchitecture and the intersection between hardware and software, with a particular focus in GPUs and how our current systems can be improved in this new age of AI and machine learning.

I'm currently finishing my last quarter at UCLA and also working as a teaching assistant for CS 132, Compiler Construction.
